about socialhub

Built for circles, not crowds

SocialHub started as a small project between friends who were tired of feeds built to maximize time-on-app instead of time-well-spent. There's no ad-driven ranking here — posts show up in the order your circle shared them.

Every account is invite-first: you build your circle deliberately, and your posts stay visible only to the people you've actually chosen to share them with.
